Our Story

Who We Are

Marquette Services is an independent, student-led organization dedicated to offering high-quality task assistance to homeowners and local businesses. Founded by motivated high school students, our mission is to balance real-world entrepreneurship with dependable neighborhood service.

We recognized that many community members need quick, reliable hands for tasks that larger contracting businesses won't take on. By organizing a structured, professional workforce of local students, we deliver outstanding results while gaining invaluable administrative and leadership skills.

Our Operating Principles

We hold our team to high professional standards to ensure peace of mind for every client we serve. Every task assigned to our workforce operates under strict principles of reliability.

Professional Integrity

We treat your property and schedule with absolute respect. Punctuality and clear communication are mandatory for every team member.

Community Supported

Choosing us directly impacts local students, providing fair compensation and foundational experience in business management.

Streamlined Structure

With simple flat-rate pricing tiers and centralized oversight, coordinating tasks is hassle-free from start to finish.
